# KFAR Marketplace - Quick Testing Guide for VOP Community

## 🚀 How to Test the Marketplace NOW

### Step 1: Open the Website
Visit: http://localhost:3000 (or the deployed URL)

### Step 2: Browse Products
1. Click "Marketplace" to see all products
2. Switch language with the עב | EN toggle
3. Search for products using the search bar
4. Filter by vendor or category

### Step 3: Add Items to Cart
1. Click any product to view details
2. Select quantity
3. Click "Add to Cart"
4. Cart icon shows item count

### Step 4: Complete Checkout
1. Click cart icon → "Proceed to Checkout"
2. Fill in your details:
   - Name: Your real name
   - Email: Your email
   - Phone: Your WhatsApp number (important!)
   - Address: Your delivery address

### Step 5: Choose Delivery
- **Pickup**: Free - Village of Peace Community Center
- **Local Delivery**: ₪15 - Within Dimona (1-2 days)
- **Standard Shipping**: ₪25 - All Israel (3-5 days)

### Step 6: Select Payment Method
- **Braysheet Tokens**: Community currency
- **Bank Transfer**: Get bank details after order
- **QR Code**: Scan with banking app
- **Credit Card**: Coming soon

### Step 7: Complete Order
1. Click "Complete Order"
2. You'll see "Order Confirmed!" with order number
3. Click "Copy Invoice Text" button
4. The invoice is now copied to your clipboard!

### Step 8: Send Invoice via WhatsApp
1. Open WhatsApp on your phone
2. Select the vendor or your own chat
3. Paste the invoice text (long press → paste)
4. Send the message

OR

Click "Share Order via WhatsApp" to open WhatsApp Web

## 📱 What the Invoice Contains
- Order number
- Date and time
- Customer details
- All items with prices
- Subtotal, delivery, tax
- Total amount to pay
- Payment method selected
- Delivery method chosen
