03 / Glass Morphism
Three Glass Tiers
Glass requires a rich background to work. Gradient orbs, mesh gradients, or vivid imagery. On white or flat grey, glass is invisible.
Tier 1: Subtle
Cards & Nav
Light background tint with minimal blur. Best for navigation bars and small cards.
background: rgba(255,255,255, 0.06);
backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.4);
border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255, 0.1);
Tier 2: Medium
Hero Cards
Stronger blur with increased saturation. Pricing cards, modals, feature highlights.
background: rgba(255,255,255, 0.08);
backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(1.8);
border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255, 0.15);
Tier 3: Liquid
Showcases
Deep blur with brightness boost and gradient background. Hero overlays.
background: linear-gradient(135deg, ...);
backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(2.0);
border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255, 0.18);

When Glass Works vs. Fails

BackgroundResultFix
Rich gradient / meshPremiumNone needed
Dark solid (#0a0a0a)ElegantAdd gradient orbs behind
Vibrant imageStunningReduce blur if image matters
White / lightInvisibleUse glass-light variant
Flat medium greyDullAdd noise or gradient

Layering Recipe

z: 0    Star field (far layer)
z: 1    Atmospheric (aurora bands, nebula clouds, rain)
z: 2    Noise overlay (0.03-0.05 opacity)
z: 3    Particles (rising, floating)
z: 10   Content (cards, text, interactive elements)

Anti-Patterns

Never Do
  • Glass on white/flat background
  • 5+ backdrop-filter elements visible
  • will-change: all (GPU memory bomb)
  • Pure #000000 background
  • Noise opacity > 0.08
  • Neon glow on light backgrounds
  • CSS animations in Remotion
Always Do
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion
  • will-change before, remove after
  • #0A0A0F minimum for dark bg
  • -webkit- prefix on backdrop-filter
  • pointer-events: none on decorative
  • aria-hidden on effect layers
  • Test at actual device size
