# Perfume Oasis African Elegance Story - Optimized Prompting Guide

## 🎯 Project Summary
Creating a luxury branded video story for Perfume Oasis featuring African actors, using FLUX Kontext for brand consistency and Hailuo AI for video generation.

## 📚 Research-Based Optimizations Applied

### 1. FLUX Kontext Best Practices
Based on extensive research, we've implemented:

- **Character Consistency**: "The same elegant African woman from scene 1 (maintaining identical features...)"
- **Clear Action Verbs**: "stands", "walks", "touches", "blends" instead of vague descriptions
- **Explicit Preservation**: "Maintain the woman's exact facial features, skin tone, hairstyle..."
- **Natural Logo Integration**: Logo appears contextually (on marble walls, garden gates, lab coats)
- **Precise Colors**: Hex codes specified (#0E5C4A emerald, #C8A95B gold)

### 2. African Representation
Authentic and respectful portrayal:

- **Specific Features**: "rich dark skin", "natural afro hair styled in an updo"
- **Cultural Elements**: Kente fabric, Adinkra symbols, baobab trees, African botanicals
- **Professional Context**: Distinguished perfumer, elegant boutique, luxury settings
- **Diverse Characters**: Woman protagonist, male master perfumer

### 3. Hailuo AI Video Optimization
Professional cinematography notation:

- **Camera Movements**: [slowly pushes in], [tracks right], [pedestals down], [slowly circles]
- **Shot Types**: "wide shot", "medium shot" clearly specified
- **Structured Narrative**: Clear beginning, middle, end for 6-second format
- **Smooth Transitions**: Specific instructions for scene flow

## 🎬 The 6-Scene Story Arc

1. **Heritage Welcome** - African woman in emerald dress welcomes to boutique
2. **Garden Discovery** - Same woman exploring African botanical ingredients
3. **Master Perfumer** - African craftsman creating fragrances
4. **Collection Reveal** - Products on kente fabric with African design
5. **Transformation** - Woman experiencing the fragrance
6. **Brand Promise** - Logo with African patterns and tagline

## 📝 Key Prompting Insights

### For FLUX Kontext:
```
✅ DO: "An elegant African woman with rich dark skin and natural afro hair..."
❌ DON'T: "A woman stands in a boutique"

✅ DO: "Maintain the woman's exact facial features throughout"
❌ DON'T: Leave consistency to chance
```

### For Hailuo AI:
```
✅ DO: "[Camera slowly pushes in] from wide shot to medium shot"
❌ DON'T: "The camera moves"

✅ DO: Structure with clear scene markers and transitions
❌ DON'T: Write one long paragraph
```
