## 🚀 Getting Started

### System Requirements
- **Desktop**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
- **Mobile**: iOS 12+, Android 8+
- **Internet**: 3G or better recommended

### First Time Setup
1. Visit [kfarmarket.com](https://kfarmarket.com)
2. Click "Join KFAR" in the top navigation
3. Choose your role: Customer or Vendor
4. Follow the onboarding process

## 👤 Customer Guide

### 1. Registration & Onboarding

#### Step 1: Create Your Account
```
1. Click "Join as Customer"
2. Enter your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Create Password
3. Verify your email
```

#### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
- **Upload Avatar**: We'll analyze it to personalize your experience
- **Set Preferences**:
  - Dietary restrictions (Vegan, Kosher, Gluten-free)
  - Favorite categories
  - Notification preferences
- **Location**: For delivery estimates

#### Step 3: Get Your QR Code
- Unique QR code generated automatically
- Download to your phone
- Use for quick checkout at vendors

### 2. Shopping Experience

#### Browsing Products
- **Search Bar**: Type product names or keywords
- **Categories**: Browse by category
- **Filters**: 
  - Price range
  - Dietary preferences
  - Vendor
  - Ratings

#### Smart Features
- **QR Scanner**: Scan product QR codes for instant info
- **Voice Search**: Tap the microphone icon
- **AI Recommendations**: Based on your preferences

#### Adding to Cart
1. Click "Add to Cart" on any product
2. Select quantity
3. View cart from bottom navigation (mobile) or header (desktop)

### 3. Checkout Process

#### Payment Methods
- Credit/Debit Cards (coming soon)
- PayPal (coming soon)
- Cash on delivery
- KFAR Credits

#### Delivery Options
- **Pickup**: From vendor location
- **Delivery**: To your address
- **Collection Point**: Community centers

### 4. Order Management

#### Track Your Orders
```
My Account → Orders → View Details
```
- Real-time status updates
- Delivery tracking
- Order history

#### Reviews & Ratings
- Rate products after delivery
- Upload photos
- Earn rewards for reviews

### 5. QR Code Features

#### Your Personal QR Code Contains:
- Customer ID
- Preference summary
- Loyalty points
- Quick checkout data

#### How to Use:
1. Show QR code at vendor
2. Vendor scans for instant recognition
3. Personalized recommendations appear
4. Express checkout available

### 6. Notifications

#### Types of Notifications:
- Order updates
- Personalized offers
- New products in your preferences
- Vendor announcements

#### Manage Notifications:
```
Profile → Settings → Notifications
```

## 🏪 Vendor Guide

### 1. Vendor Registration

#### Requirements:
- Business registration details
- Product catalog (can be added later)
- Bank account for payments
- Contact information

#### Onboarding Steps:
1. Go to "Become a Vendor"
2. Fill business information
3. Upload documents
4. Wait for approval (24-48 hours)
5. Set up your store

### 2. Store Setup

#### Customize Your Storefront:
- **Logo**: 500x500px recommended
- **Banner**: 1200x400px recommended
- **Description**: Tell your story
- **Operating Hours**: Set availability
- **Delivery Options**: Configure methods

### 3. Product Management

#### Adding Products:

**Method 1: Single Product**
```
Dashboard → Products → Add New
- Name & Description
- Price & Currency
- Images (multiple)
- Category & Tags
- Inventory status
```

**Method 2: Bulk Upload**
```
Dashboard → Products → Import CSV
- Download template
- Fill product details
- Upload CSV file
- Review and confirm
```

#### Product Images:
- **Main Image**: 800x800px minimum
- **Additional**: Up to 5 images
- **Formats**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **AI Verification**: Automatic quality check

### 4. Order Management

#### Order Workflow:
1. **New Order** → Notification received
2. **Review** → Check items and delivery
3. **Confirm** → Accept or reject
4. **Prepare** → Update status
5. **Complete** → Mark as ready/delivered

#### Order Dashboard Features:
- Real-time order feed
- Customer details
- Special instructions
- Print packing lists

### 5. Customer Insights

#### QR Scanner Dashboard:
- Scan customer QR codes
- View preferences instantly
- See purchase history
- Offer personalized deals

#### Analytics Available:
- Sales trends
- Popular products
- Customer demographics
- Peak hours
- Revenue reports

### 6. Marketing Tools

#### Promotions:
- Create discount codes
- Flash sales
- Bundle offers
- Loyalty rewards

#### Customer Communication:
- Send updates to followers
- Announce new products
- Share your story
- Build community

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues:

#### Can't Login:
- Check email/password
- Clear browser cache
- Try password reset
- Contact support

#### QR Code Not Working:
- Ensure good lighting
- Clean camera lens
- Update app
- Regenerate QR code

#### Payment Failed:
- Check card details
- Sufficient balance
- Try different method
- Contact bank

#### Slow Loading:
- Check internet connection
- Clear app cache
- Update browser
- Try mobile data
